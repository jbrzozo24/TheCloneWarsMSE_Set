Fixing the Jedi creature curve

Proposal for review · The Clone Wars v3 · 2026-10-13 · revision 3: Waxer trade dropped, Anakin trade added (2/2, LLL) · applied 2026-11-07 with Rex flip 3G and Jedi Sacrifice 2GG

Recommendation: make four mana-value trades, each between two cards of the same colour and rarity. In each trade, a Jedi creature costs less and its partner costs more, and both are rebalanced to match. This brings Jedi creatures at 4 mana down from 8 to 4. The set's colour, rarity and colour×rarity curves stay exactly the same.

The problem

Playtesters found that Jedi decks had nothing to do early. The count bears it out. Counting every creature with the Jedi subtype or a Jedi archetype tag in the sheet, across all colours, gives exactly 8 at mana value 4:

Green-blue's overall creature curve is normal: 18 / 20 / 16 copies at 2 / 3 / 4, in line with the other colour pairs. The problem is that the Jedi-flavoured bodies pile up at 4. At 2 mana there are only two: Sparring Padawan and Maintenance Trooper.

Approach

  • Trade mana values within colour and rarity. If a green rare drops from 4 to 3, another green rare rises from 3 to 4. Every colour × rarity × MV bucket keeps its count, so the colour and rarity curves you built don't move.
  • Partners are mostly non-creatures (a sorcery, a battle, a craft, two instants). The creature curve gets earlier without the colour's total curve changing.
  • Rebalance both sides. Cheaper creatures lose a bit of stats. Pricier partners gain a clear upgrade, so they're still worth playing.
  • Archetype tags are a soft constraint. As you said, cards are meant to cross archetypes, so tag-curve shifts are listed below for reference, not as blockers.

The four trades

1 · Jedi Youngling Initiate ⇄ Commune with Bounty Hunters

green common · 3 ⇄ 2
▼ 3 → 2

CG06 Jedi Youngling Initiate

2G → 1G · Creature — Human Jedi · 2/3 → 2/2
  • When this creature enters, you get LL.
  • LL: Untap target creature you control.

Rules text unchanged. The Jedi's only common creature becomes a 2-drop that gives you L on turn 2. Losing 1 toughness pays for the cheaper cost.

▲ 2 → 3

CG11 Commune with Bounty Hunters

1G → 2G · Sorcery
  • Look at the top four five cards of your library. You may reveal an outlaw card or and/or a land card from among them and put them into your hand. Put the rest into your graveyard.

One more card seen, and it can now find two cards. That makes it a real 3-mana card-advantage spell for Bounty Hunters and graveyard decks.

2 · Anakin Skywalker, 501st General ⇄ Twice the Pride, Double the Fall

green-white uncommon · 4 ⇄ 2

Twice the Pride is the only other green-white uncommon, so an exact trade has to be 4 ⇄ 2. Both are Republic cards that Jedi decks can also play.

▼ 4 → 2

UZ09 Anakin Skywalker, 501st General

2GW → GW · Legendary Creature — Jedi General · 4/3 → 2/2
  • When Anakin enters, you get L for each Clone you control.
  • Whenever Anakin attacks with two or more creatures, you may pay LL LLL. If you do, put a +1/+1 counter on each Clone you control.

Final version, per your review: 2/2, and the Clone pump costs LLL. His enters ability gives little on turn 2, but later he's a cheap way to refill L. The pricier attack trigger keeps a 2-mana legend from snowballing go-wide Clone decks too fast.

▲ 2 → 4

UZ15 Twice the Pride, Double the Fall

{G/W}{G/W} → 2{G/W}{G/W} · Battle — Duel (defense 6)
  • Duel. When this card enters, draw a card. …if your opponent has more cards than you, or Then if an opponent controls more lands than you, search your library for a basic land card, put it onto the battlefield tapped, then shuffle.

It used to do one conditional thing. Now it always draws, and it also ramps if you're behind on lands. Hybrid mana keeps it castable in either colour.

3 · Obi-Wan and Captain Rex ⇄ Republic Gunship and Jedi Sacrifice

green rare · 4, 4 ⇄ 3, 2

Green rares have 2 / 2 / 3 cards at mana values 2 / 3 / 4. Moving Obi-Wan to 3 and Rex to 2, then the Gunship to 4 and Jedi Sacrifice to 4, leaves those counts exactly as they are.

▼ 4 → 3

RG02 Obi-Wan Kenobi, Jedi Master

2GG → 1GG · Legendary Creature — Human Jedi · 3/4 → 3/3
  • Trample, vigilance
  • When Obi-Wan enters or blocks, you may pay LL any number of times. Each time you do, put a +1/+1 counter on each untapped creature you control.
  • 1U, T: Untap target permanent you control.

His enter and block ability scales with your L, so he's still strong later. Losing trample and a point of toughness pays for the cheaper cost.

▲ 3 → 4

RG03 Republic Gunship

1GG → 2GG · Legendary Artifact — Craft Gunship 3/3
  • When Republic Gunship enters, create a two 1/1 white Clone Trooper creature tokens.
  • Flying, scrap 2. Whenever Republic Gunship attacks, you may pay 2. If you do, put target Clone creature card from your graveyard onto the battlefield tapped and attacking.

The second token also feeds scrap 2, so the Gunship becomes a creature as soon as it's cast.

▼ 4 → 2

RG08 Captain Rex, Loyal Soldier

3G → 1G · Legendary Creature — Clone Commander · 3/3 → 2/2
  • When Rex enters, you get LLL LL.
  • Vigilance, reach
  • 1G 3G: Transform Rex. Activate only as a sorcery and only if a clone you control died this turn.

A 2-drop that gives you L, and it helps both Jedi and Republic. The transform now costs 3G, so the 5/5 card-drawing back face can't show up on turn 3. The back face is unchanged.

▲ 2 → 4

RG06 Jedi Sacrifice

1G → 2GG · Instant
  • Target creature you control gets +2/+2 and gains indestructible and “When another creature you control would be dealt damage, you may have this creature be dealt that damage instead” until end of turn. Draw a card.

It's now a combat blowout: one indestructible creature soaks all the damage, and it still draws a card. That's worth 4 mana at rare.

4 · Jedi Temple Guard ⇄ Take a Seat

blue rare · 4 ⇄ 3
▼ 4 → 3

RU03 Jedi Temple Guard

3U → 2U · Creature — Jedi Soldier · 2/2 → 1/2
  • Jedi Temple Guard can’t be blocked.
  • U, T: Another target creature you control can’t be blocked this turn.
  • Whenever one or more creatures you control deal combat damage to one or more players, draw a card.

Rules text unchanged. An unblockable creature that draws cards is strong at 3, so it drops to 1 power.

▲ 3 → 4

RU06 Take a Seat

2U → 3U · Instant
  • Tap target creature. It doesn’t untap during its controller’s next untap step. That creature’s controller discards a card. Draw a card.

One creature locked for a turn, you draw a card and they discard one. That's fair at 4.

Questions for you

  1. Anakin's cost: GW (as proposed) or hybrid {G/W}{G/W}? Hybrid would make him castable in mono-green Jedi decks, not just green-white.
  2. Waxer's name (separate from this proposal): fix the “Clone Liutenant Waxer” spelling some other time?
  3. Rex's transform cost: 3G is a cautious choice. 2G would make the flip easier to reach, since a 2-drop Rex is often on the battlefield early.
  4. Jedi Sacrifice: 2GG or 3G? 2GG keeps it heavily green. 3G is easier to splash.
  5. Any trade you'd rather skip? Each one stands alone. Dropping one simply leaves its two cards as they are.
